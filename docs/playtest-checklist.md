# 10-minute playtest checklist (real devices)

For the things the test machines can't do: real YouTube, iPhones and iPads, real network lag. Two or
three people, at least one on an iPhone or iPad, one on a laptop. Each step says what you should see.

## 1. Join (1 min)

1. Everyone opens the site, enters the passphrase, and joins one room. *Each name appears in the
   lobby with a green dot.*
2. On the iPhone, swipe back (or press the browser's Back) from the lobby. *The start page shows
   "Back to your game · room ABCD". Tap it: you're back in your seat.*

## 2. Music (3 min)

1. On the laptop, open Music and paste a YouTube **video** link. *It plays on every screen at about
   the same point (within a couple of seconds). A screen that hasn't been tapped yet shows "Click
   to join the music"; one tap and it joins in step.*
2. Paste a link to a video you opened **from a playlist or a Mix** (the address has `&list=`).
   *That one song goes on the queue, not the whole playlist.*
3. Paste a **playlist** of short songs (`/playlist?list=`), skip to it, and let its first song
   play to the end. *When YouTube moves on to the playlist's next song, every screen moves on
   with it, and the name shown is the new song's.*
4. On the iPhone, open Music. *It says to use the phone's volume buttons (iPhones don't let a page
   change YouTube's volume). Tick "Mute for me": the music pauses on that phone only. Untick it:
   it comes back at the same point as everyone else.*
5. On the laptop, drag the volume slider. *It changes at once, only on the laptop.*
6. Press **Stop**. *The music stops for everyone and goes back to the start of the song; the queue
   is still there. Press Play: it starts again for everyone.*
7. Press **Clear the queue**, then **Yes, clear the queue**. *Only the songs waiting are gone.*

## 3. A game on real devices (5 min)

1. Start a Full game. On the laptop (a 1366×768 screen or a small browser window), look at the
   players box. *If someone doesn't fit, their name and points show at the foot of the box; tap it
   to scroll to them.*
2. Each person picks a different board style in My settings, including **Pixel** and **Pikmin**.
   *Every harbor is visible in every style; no lily pad or boat sits under one. Every number has
   its dots (pips). On a fog map, fog looks like fog (a cloud), not like land.*
3. On the iPhone, turn on airplane mode for 10 seconds during someone else's turn, then off. *A
   "reconnecting" note shows, then the game catches up by itself; nothing is lost.*
4. On a slow connection (phone on mobile data, or the laptop browser's network throttling set to
   "Slow 3G"), tap Roll twice quickly and Buy a card twice quickly. *One roll; one card per tap
   that you could afford, never two for one tap.*
5. In the lobby before the next game, tap the points − button three times quickly and tick two
   house rules quickly. *All of them stick, on every screen.*

## If something's off

Note the time, the device, and what you saw; a screenshot helps. The server keeps every move, so a
time and a room code are enough to look it up.
